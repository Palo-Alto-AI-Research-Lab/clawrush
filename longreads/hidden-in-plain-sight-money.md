# The Influence Is Not the Pyramids. I Think It Is Money.

Some thoughts on our development as Homo sapiens.

I believe extraterrestrial influence exists. I am saying that plainly, because I would rather be honest about what I actually think than sound respectable.

Whether that influence is extraterrestrial or intraterrestrial is a completely different question, and I do not have the answer. Maybe someone came from outside. Maybe there was another civilization here before us, on this planet, one that lived and died and left something behind. I do not know which. Nobody has shown me proof of either, and I am not going to pretend I have it.

But here is the part I keep coming back to. It does not actually matter who did the influencing. The influence itself was there. Something happened to us.

And the most interesting thing is not what happened thousands of years ago. It is the influence you can see right now, in the civilization we are living in today.

It is not the pyramids. It is not Machu Picchu. It is not polygonal masonry in some ancient megalithic wall.

Everyone looks there. Everyone points at the stones.

The influence that is present in our civilization right now is something far, far more obvious than that.

There is an old line about this: if you want to hide something, put it on the surface. Put it where everyone walks past it every single day and never stops.

So here is my guess. Money. The monetary system. That is the biggest influence that ever showed up in this species. Or, if not money itself, then the customization of money, the moment it stopped being a thing you could weigh and became a thing you had to believe in.

Objects of exchange, barter, tokens, all of that could have been invented by Homo sapiens on our own. Probably was. But the system that grew on top of it does not behave like a tool we picked up. It behaves like something that shapes us back.

Am I sure? No. I do not know.

I am not writing this to convince you. I am writing it because the habit of only looking at the spectacular stuff, the stones, the sky, the artifacts behind museum glass, is exactly how you miss the thing sitting on the table in front of you. And because I would rather put a raw thought out in public and get argued with than keep it polished and private.

If you think I am wrong, tell me why. That is genuinely more useful to me than agreement.
