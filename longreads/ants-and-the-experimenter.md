# If You Wanted Ants to Reach Space, You Would Give Them Money

This continues the thought about Homo sapiens and outside influence. [Part one is here.](hidden-in-plain-sight-money.md)

I think money, our current money system, is an invention made not by people, but by whoever civilized them.

Here is the picture I keep turning over. Suppose you are a scientist running an experiment. You have an anthill, and you want the ants to fly to space.

The ants are lazy. They do not want to do anything. They are lying in the sun and everything is fine with them. So you need to give them some kind of magic pill that makes the ants suddenly, sharply want to build spaceships.

And if you break a spaceship down into steps, there are an enormous number of them before you get there, starting with inventing the semiconductor and going on from there. You need these little ants to start understanding physics, chemistry, and a lot of other things.

So you say to the little ants: there is a new entity in your game now. Money. Whoever works the most, or whoever is craftier and out-tricks everyone else, earns the most. And you can become anything you like: a scientist, a government worker, anything.

My point is simply this. Show people money, and people are forced to achieve something. And progress runs in every direction, including wars, which possibly also push these little ants toward achievements.

Okay, moving on.

The little ants develop this way. They start flying to space, making semiconductors. It is just an experiment. It is what you, the experimenter, wanted in the first place.

Why should we, humans, not be exactly the same little ants? Well, anything is possible.

And do the little ants see the experimenter? Most likely not. We are in no way manifested to them. They do not even react to us. They have not gathered for a war against us.

The experimenter who sets black ants against red ones, and those against white ones, is simply invisible to them. It is as if he is located in another dimension.

Therefore... think the rest through yourself.
