# Wild Thoughts in My Head

About people. And replaceability.

One fewer person is fifteen hundred a month. And I immediately think: right, for fifteen hundred you could buy a certain number of tokens in Claude.

And so I think: which is more profitable, one fewer person at fifteen hundred, or Claude for the same money?

The scale is tipped toward Claude so far.

Horror stories like that. If you are a crazy manager who has learned to use Claude, think about it.

Although, honestly, this is of course awful. Because on the one hand I really love and value all my people. And on the other, when you have to survive, you end up cutting people against your own will.

A bit later I will write about how my Claude fired an employee by itself.
